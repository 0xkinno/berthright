# Threat model: the berth hand-over

Written before the contract code, revised after the sandbox tests. A berth is a House of Stake
leased name: an account that is also an NEP-171 token. The question the NFT track asks is the one
this file answers: when the token moves, what stops anyone from keeping or diverting the money?

Actors: the outgoing crew member (seller), the relief (buyer), the shipowner, the crew welfare trust
(namespace issuer), House of Stake (registry council), the relayer, and anyone calling the crank.

| # | Threat | Who | What stops it | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | The old holder keeps acting on the berth after hand-over | seller | Every rotation clears all extensions except the HoS authority and inserts the new owner (`apply_rotation`). The berth has no access key, so there is nothing else to keep. | `tla_transfer_wipes_the_allotment_grant`, `add_key_on_a_berth_is_impossible_and_house_of_stake_cannot_move_it_alone` |
| T2 | The old holder's allotment keeps draining the berth | seller | The grant is wiped by the wallet at rotation; our allotment also re-reads `nft_item_info` and stops on a changed `rotation_seq` (`E_BERTH_CHANGED_HANDS`). Two independent locks. | `allotment_stops_after_hand_over` |
| T3 | The chest keeps paying wages into a berth the old holder no longer holds | seller, buyer | `settle` re-reads the berth every time. If the owner or `rotation_seq` changed without a sign-off, wages go to the account that signed on, the berth becomes `Interrupted` and accrual stops. | `an_unannounced_hand_over_pays_the_recorded_holder_and_never_the_berth`, `an_unannounced_hand_over_on_chain_pays_arjun_not_the_relief` |
| T4 | The buyer receives tokens the seller earned | buyer | HoS does not sweep NEP-141 balances at transfer (verified, `docs/FINDINGS.md`). Our hand-over flow therefore signs off first (final wages to the seller's own account) and moves anything left in the berth to the seller, in the same approval as the transfer. | `tla_transfer_sweeps_native_near_to_the_seller_but_leaves_tokens`, `sign_off_then_hand_over_pays_arjun_and_lets_the_relief_sign_on` |
| T5 | The seller sweeps native NEAR the buyer paid into the berth | seller | HoS sends native NEAR above the storage reserve to the outgoing payout account at transfer. Berthright never stores wages as native NEAR; the app warns before any NEAR is sent to a berth. | `tla_transfer_sweeps_native_near_to_the_seller_but_leaves_tokens` |
| T6 | The sweep fails and NEAR is stuck or lost | anyone | The sweep is detached; on failure HoS emits `SweepFailed` and the NEAR stays in the berth for the new owner. Not lost, but it changes hands. Documented, not relied on. | `a_failed_sweep_leaves_the_near_for_the_new_owner` |
| T7 | A settle reads the berth, then it changes hands, then the payment lands | seller, buyer | Cross-contract reads can be stale (sharded-item draft). The window is the blocks between the view and the callback, and the amount is one settlement. The app signs off before the hand-over. Residual risk accepted and stated in `docs/RISK.md`. | design note |
| T8 | The shipowner takes the reserve back | shipowner | `withdraw_surplus` books every berth's wages first and refuses any amount above the unreserved pool, any withdrawal while wages are unpaid, and any withdrawal after abandonment. | `the_owner_cannot_withdraw_the_reserve` |
| T9 | The shipowner seizes or transfers a berth | shipowner | The shipowner never holds a berth. A live lease cannot rotate without the holder asking (`needs_holder`). | `add_key_on_a_berth_is_impossible_and_house_of_stake_cannot_move_it_alone` |
| T10 | The shipowner signs a crew member on at a lower wage than the contract | shipowner | `sign_on` requires the crew member's own confirmed wage and the SEA hash to equal the berth's terms. | `sign_on_is_refused_when_the_wage_differs_from_the_crew_members_contract` |
| T11 | The shipowner delays abandonment by topping up a token amount | shipowner | Funding first pays every unpaid wage; the arrears clock only resets when all arrears are covered. Partial top-ups do not reset it. | `funding_after_arrears_pays_the_crew_first_and_stops_the_clock` |
| T12 | Someone releases the guarantee twice, or to themselves | anyone | Release is once per berth (`E_ALREADY_RELEASED`) and pays only the recorded holder. Callers choose nothing. | `release_guarantee_pays_once` |
| T13 | Release pays more than is owed | anyone | Release pays earmarked wages + min(reserve wage part, arrears + remaining contract wages) + allowance. The rest of the reserve returns to the ship. | `release_never_pays_more_than_is_owed` |
| T14 | A fake berth (a lookalike account) signs on | anyone | Berths must end with `.{registrar}`, and sign-on needs `nft_item_info` and the registry's `nft_token` to agree on collection, token id and owner. Only the registrar can create names under it. | `a_berth_must_be_a_name_in_the_crew_namespace`, `a_non_holder_cannot_sign_on` |
| T15 | The trust (namespace issuer) moves a live berth | trust | On our fleet the trust is a payment authority on an Open namespace: it issues names and holds no transfer or freeze power. A licensee-governed business namespace would have more power; see FRICTION.md. | design note |
| T16 | House of Stake moves a berth | HoS council | HoS recovery and reclaim exist (lease expiry, recovery quorum). A live lease cannot be transferred by HoS without the holder. A berth lease outlasts the crewing contract by design. Stated in RISK.md. | HoS `apply_rotation` |
| T17 | The relayer spends on its own behalf | relayer | The relayer only wraps signed delegate actions to an allowlist of receivers and methods, with zero deposit except the 1 yocto berth calls, and per-IP caps. It never signs a transaction of its own choosing. | `app/app/api/relay/route.ts` |
| T18 | A token transfer to the berth or holder fails | anyone | Every payout registers the receiver first; a failed transfer puts the wages back on board (`payment_failed`) and the next settle retries. | `a_failed_payment_puts_the_wages_back_on_board` |
| T19 | The crew member's phone is stolen in port | thief | The holder freezes the berth (`hos_freeze`); nothing leaves it while frozen, wages keep arriving. | `a_frozen_berth_does_not_lose_wages` |
