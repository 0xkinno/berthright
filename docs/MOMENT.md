# The moment

**Arjun Nair (fictional), 31, able seaman from Kochi, Kerala.** Day 74 at anchor off Khor Fakkan, UAE,
21:40 ship's time. His company has not paid wages for two months. Under the Maritime Labour
Convention, two months unpaid already counts as abandonment. His sister's college fee is due on
Friday. The ship's insurer is meant to cover up to four months of wages, but first he has to find out
who the insurer is, file a claim from a ship with patchy internet, and wait.

Arjun is fictional. Everything below is not.
